How to Replace a Window Screen

Short answer: Pop out the old rubber spline, lay new screen mesh over the frame, and press it into the channel with a spline roller and new spline, working around the frame while keeping the mesh taut. Trim the excess with a utility knife.

Difficulty
Easy
Time
30 to 45 minutes per screen

What is causing it?

Screens tear from pets, kids, weather and age. The aluminum frame is usually fine; only the mesh and the rubber spline that holds it need replacing.

How do you replace a window screen? Step by step

  1. Remove the screen and old spline

    Take the screen out of the window, lay it flat, and lift one end of the spline with a screwdriver, then pull it out of the channel. Remove the old mesh.

  2. Lay the new mesh

    Cut the mesh about 1 inch larger than the frame on all sides and lay it square over the frame. Clamp the edges if it shifts.

  3. Roll in two adjacent sides

    Start in a corner. Use the convex wheel to push the mesh into the channel, then the concave wheel to press the spline in. Do one long side and one short side.

  4. Keep it taut

    Family Handyman suggests putting a weight in the middle of the screen before rolling the remaining sides, which helps keep the mesh tight without bowing the frame.

  5. Finish and trim

    Roll in the last two sides. If you see wrinkles, pull that section of spline and roll it again. Trim the excess mesh along the outside edge of the spline with a sharp blade angled away from the screen.

Safety notes

  • Remove screens from upper floors from inside the house when possible; do not lean out windows.
  • Change to a fresh knife blade so it cuts cleanly rather than slipping.

When should you call a pro?

  • The frame is bent or the corners are broken; a new frame may be simpler.
  • The screen is part of a specialty window or retractable system.

Frequently asked questions

Fiberglass or aluminum screen?

Fiberglass is easier to work with and will not dent. Aluminum is stiffer and more durable but kinks easily during installation.

How do I know what spline size to buy?

Match the old spline's diameter, or measure the channel width. Packages list the sizes.

Why is my new screen loose?

The mesh was not held taut while rolling. Pull the spline on one side and re-roll it.
