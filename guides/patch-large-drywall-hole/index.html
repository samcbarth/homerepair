How to Patch a Large Hole in Drywall

Short answer: Cut the damage into a clean square, screw wood backing strips inside the opening, screw in a matching piece of drywall, then tape the seams and finish with three thin coats of joint compound before priming and painting.

Difficulty
Moderate
Time
2 hours of work over 1 to 2 days
Updated
November 9, 2026

What is causing it?

Holes larger than about 6 inches are too wide for a stick-on patch. They need a new piece of drywall supported by backing so it does not flex and crack the finish.

How do you patch a large drywall hole? Step by step

  1. Check behind the wall

    Look into the hole with a flashlight for wires, pipes and ducts before cutting. Turn off the breaker for nearby outlets if wires are close.

  2. Cut a clean square

    Draw a square around the damage and cut it out with a jab saw, keeping the saw shallow.

  3. Add backing

    Slip a 1x3 strip inside the opening so it overlaps the edge, and screw through the drywall into it at each end. This Old House shows backing that overlaps into the hole by about 1 1/4 inches. Add a strip on the opposite side.

  4. Fit the patch

    Cut a drywall piece to fit with about 1/8 inch gap around it. Screw it to the backing strips with the screw heads just below the surface without tearing the paper.

  5. Tape and first coat

    Spread compound over the seams, press tape into it, and scrape off the excess. Cover the screw heads.

  6. Finish coats

    After it dries, apply two more coats, each wider and thinner, feathering several inches past the seams. Sand smooth between coats.

  7. Prime and paint

    Prime the whole patch area and paint, rolling wider than the patch to blend.

Safety notes

  • Wiring and plumbing run inside walls. Look before you cut and keep cuts shallow.
  • In homes built before 1978, painted surfaces may contain lead. Follow EPA lead-safe practices for cutting and sanding.
  • Wear eye protection and a dust mask.

When should you call a pro?

  • The hole was caused by a leak, or the framing behind it is wet, soft or moldy.
  • Wiring was damaged.
  • The wall is plaster on lath, which needs different materials.

Frequently asked questions

What is a California patch?

A drywall piece cut larger than the hole with the gypsum scraped off the edges so the paper forms a flange. It works without backing for holes up to about 6 inches square.

Mesh or paper tape?

Mesh is self-adhesive and easy for beginners. Paper tape is stronger for seams and corners but must be bedded in compound.

How do I match wall texture?

Spray texture cans for orange peel or knockdown are sold at hardware stores. Practice on cardboard first.
