Doors and Windows repairs you can do yourself

Doors and windows take daily abuse. Squeaks, sticking, latches that miss and drafts usually come from loose hinge screws, seasonal swelling or worn seals, and all have simple fixes.

What doors and windows jobs are safe to DIY?

Tightening hinges, lubricating, adjusting strike plates, planing a sticking edge, weatherstripping and rescreening are all DIY friendly. A door or window that suddenly binds along with new wall cracks can point to settling; have it inspected.
