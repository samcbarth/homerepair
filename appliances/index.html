Appliances repairs you can do yourself

Before you book a repair visit, a few owner-level checks solve a lot of appliance problems. These guides follow the manufacturers' own troubleshooting steps.

Safety first

Unplug the appliance or turn off its breaker before reaching inside. Never put your hand into a garbage disposal. Sealed refrigerant, gas and internal electrical repairs are for a qualified technician.

What appliances jobs are safe to DIY?

Clearing jams, cleaning filters, coils and vents, and checking hoses are owner-level maintenance in most manuals. Replacing motors, control boards, compressors or gas parts is technician work.
