Basic Home Toolkit: The Tools That Handle Most Repairs

Short answer: A drill/driver, a screwdriver set, adjustable wrench, tongue-and-groove pliers, a tape measure, a utility knife, a non-contact voltage tester, a flange plunger, putty knives, a level, a stepladder and a flashlight cover most of the repairs on this site. Buy those first and add specialty tools as jobs come up.

Frequently asked questions

What tools should every homeowner have?

A drill/driver, screwdrivers, adjustable wrench, pliers, utility knife, tape measure, voltage tester, plungers, putty knives, a stepladder and a flashlight cover the most common repairs.

Should I buy a tool kit or individual tools?

Pre-made kits are convenient but often include low-quality filler. Buying the core tools one at a time usually gets better quality where it matters.

Do I need a voltage tester if I turn off the breaker?

Yes. Breaker labels are often wrong and boxes can contain more than one circuit. Always verify at the device.
