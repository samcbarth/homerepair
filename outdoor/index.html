Outdoor repairs you can do yourself

Outdoor maintenance keeps water out of the house. Clean gutters, tight spigots and drained hose bibs prevent the expensive repairs that start with a leak or a frozen pipe.

Safety first

Ladder work causes many serious home injuries. Use a sound ladder on level ground, keep three points of contact, and look for power lines before you raise it.

What outdoor jobs are safe to DIY?

Gutter cleaning from a ladder, spigot repairs and winterizing are common homeowner jobs. Roof work, two-story gutters, and burst pipes inside walls are good times to call a pro.
