Plumbing repairs you can do yourself

Most plumbing problems in a house come down to a worn rubber part, a clog, or a loose connection. These guides walk through the fixes that need only hand tools and a shutoff valve.

Safety first

Know where your main water shutoff is before you start any plumbing job. Gas water heater problems beyond checking the pilot belong to a licensed plumber or gas technician.

What plumbing jobs are safe to DIY?

Replacing flappers, fill valves, faucet cartridges, aerators and P-traps, and clearing clogs with a plunger or hand auger are all reasonable DIY jobs. Moving pipes, adding fixtures, sewer line problems, and gas connections usually need a licensed plumber and often a permit.
