Walls and Tile repairs you can do yourself

Drywall dents, holes, nail pops and tired bathroom caulk are the most common cosmetic repairs in a house. All of them are learnable with a putty knife, patience and thin coats.

Safety first

Homes built before 1978 may have lead paint. Do not dry-sand or scrape old painted surfaces until you know; follow EPA lead-safe practices or test first.

What walls jobs are safe to DIY?

Patching holes, fixing nail pops, recaulking and regrouting are classic DIY jobs. Large cracks that keep coming back, sagging ceilings, or water stains that spread point to structural or leak problems that need a pro.
