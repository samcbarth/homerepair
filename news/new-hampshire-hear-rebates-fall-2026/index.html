New Hampshire's home electrification rebates expected to launch in fall 2026

Published July 29, 2026 · Source: NH Department of Energy

The short version: New Hampshire's Department of Energy says its HEAR program, with rebates of up to $14,000 for heat pumps, heat pump water heaters, wiring and insulation, is expected to launch in fall 2026. Only projects started after launch will qualify.

What happened

New Hampshire's Department of Energy says its Home Electrification and Appliance Rebates (HEAR) program is still under development and is expected to launch in fall 2026. The state approved its implementation contract with Everblue on March 25, 2026. The program page was last updated July 29, 2026.

Listed maximum rebates include $8,000 for a heat pump for space heating and cooling, $1,750 for a heat pump water heater, $840 each for an electric stove or heat pump clothes dryer, $4,000 for an electric panel upgrade, $2,500 for electrical wiring, and $1,600 for insulation, air sealing and ventilation, with a $14,000 cap per household.

Rebates are limited to households earning up to 150 percent of area median income. Households under 80 percent can receive up to 100 percent of project costs, and households between 80 and 150 percent up to 50 percent, subject to the caps. The department says rebates will only be available for projects started after the program officially launches.

Why it matters if you fix things at home

If you are in New Hampshire and planning a water heater or HVAC replacement, the start date matters: work begun before launch will not qualify. Other states run their own programs on different timelines, so check your state energy office. For a failing tank today, start with our water heater troubleshooting guide.
