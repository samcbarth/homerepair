DREO recalls about 338,800 DR-HSH015 ceramic space heaters for fire risk

Published October 29, 2026 · Source: CPSC

The short version: DREO recalled about 338,800 DR-HSH015 ceramic space heaters made from April 2025 through January 2026 because they can overheat and ignite. Stop using them and contact DREO for a free replacement.

What happened

The CPSC announced on October 29, 2026 that DREO is recalling about 338,800 DR-HSH015 ceramic space heaters in the U.S., plus about 36,300 in Canada. The heaters can overheat and ignite, posing fire and burn hazards.

Only units manufactured from April 2025 through January 2026 are included. The model number and the manufacture date (YYYY/MM) are printed on a label on the underside. The black heaters measure about 11.5 inches wide by 13 inches tall and came with a remote. They sold at Costco and online retailers including Dreo.com, Lowes.com and HomeDepot.com from September 2025 through October 2026 for about $70 to $110.

DREO has received 17 reports of heaters igniting, three with property damage and one burn injury, per the notice. Consumers are asked to unplug the heater, cut the cord, send a photo of the destroyed unit, and receive a free replacement. Contact DREO at 833-820-0887 or through the product recalls link at dreo.com.

Why it matters if you fix things at home

Space heaters are a common cause of tripped breakers and home fires in winter. Plug heaters directly into a wall outlet, keep them away from anything that can burn, and if a heater keeps tripping a breaker, see our breaker guide before resetting it again.
