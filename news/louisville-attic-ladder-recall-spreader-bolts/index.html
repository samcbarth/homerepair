Over one million attic stairway ladders recalled over breaking bolts

Published August 6, 2026 · Source: CPSC

The short version: Louisville Ladder recalled more than one million Louisville, Featherlite, Lite and Century attic stairway ladders with gas struts because bolts can break. Owners can register for a free repair kit with replacement spreader bolts.

What happened

The CPSC announced on August 6, 2026 that Louisville Ladder is recalling over one million attic stairway ladders sold under the Louisville, Featherlite, Lite and Century names that use gas struts.

According to the notice, bolts on the ladders can break, posing a fall hazard. The firm has received 11 reports of a bolt breaking, including one report of an injury to the neck, head and back.

The remedy is a free repair kit that includes two replacement 1/4 inch spreader bolts, washers and lock nuts with installation instructions. The brand and model number are located on the inside of the attic door.

Why it matters if you fix things at home

Attic ladders get used for holiday decorations, HVAC filters and checking for roof leaks, often while carrying something. Check the label inside your attic door and register for the kit if your model is listed.
