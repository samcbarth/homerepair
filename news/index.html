Little Giant recalls about 3.66 million ladders with Rapid Locks over fall hazard

The CPSC and Little Giant announced a recall of about 3.66 million ladders with Rapid Locks because they can suddenly retract if the locks are not fully engaged. Owners should stop using them and contact Little Giant for a repair kit.
